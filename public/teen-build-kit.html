Questions

Can my teen use it on their own?

No. Claude accounts are for adults (18+). You own the account, run setup and are the game's safety moderator; your teen designs and builds alongside you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English, and the setup helper tells you exactly where to click when it needs you.

Does this make Roblox safe?

No product can promise that. The kit adds protection inside the game you build and gives you tools to notice and report problems. Keep using Roblox's own parental controls and stay involved.

What computer do I need?

A Mac (macOS 13 or newer) or a Windows 10 or 11 PC. It doesn't run on Chromebooks, iPads or phones, though the games you make can be played on phones.

How do refunds work?

Reply to your payment receipt within 7 days for a full refund. No form, no questions.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. It's an independent kit that works with Roblox Studio and Claude Code, sold through ASH COW.

Which kit is right for us?

Building with a child under 13? The Safe Build Kit is made for them. See the Safe Build Kit.
