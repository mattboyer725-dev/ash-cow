What still stays locked

Roblox already age-checks chat and filters messages. This kit adds a second layer inside your game, plus rules that stop Claude from building risky features.

What it does

  • Blocks phone numbers, addresses, emails and school; moving chats to Discord, Snapchat or other apps; "where do you live" and meet-ups; secrecy, "are you alone" and picture requests, including disguised spellings
  • Gifts between players are blocked; trades need something on both sides, and lopsided trades alert you
  • Repeat offenders are paused and remembered across servers
  • An always-visible "Report a problem" button, a private incident log and live alerts to you
  • Claude won't build private chat, private rooms, romance or dating, gambling, loot boxes, or anything that asks for personal details

What it doesn't do

  • Replace your supervision or Roblox's own protections
  • Hear voice chat or see images
  • Catch every trick, though it learns new ones as it goes
  • Ban anyone automatically. A person decides.

Questions

Can my teen use it on their own?

No. Claude accounts are for adults (18+). You own the account, run setup and are the game's safety moderator; your teen designs and builds alongside you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English and the helper tells you exactly where to click when it needs you.

Does this make Roblox safe?

No product can promise that. The kit adds protection inside the game you build together, and tools to notice and report problems. Keep using Roblox's own controls and stay involved.

Which kit is right for us?

Building with a child under 13? Get the Safe Build Kit instead. Safe Build Kit

What computer do I need?

A Mac (macOS 13 or newer) or a Windows 10/11 PC. Not Chromebooks, iPads or phones, though the games you make can be played on phones.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. It's an independent kit that works with Roblox Studio and Claude Code, sold through ASH COW.
