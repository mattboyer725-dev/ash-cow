Safety, built in from the start

Roblox already age-checks chat and filters messages. This kit adds a second layer inside your game, plus rules that stop the AI from building risky features in the first place.

What it adds

  • Blocks messages sharing phone numbers, addresses or other apps, or asking where someone lives or how old they are
  • An always-visible “Report a problem” button kids can use in three taps
  • A private log of anything flagged, and alerts to you while you're in the game
  • Plain-English guidance on what to do, including who to report to
  • The AI won't build private chat, item gifting, secluded rooms or anything that asks kids for personal details

What it doesn't do

  • Replace your supervision or Roblox's own protections
  • Hear voice chat or see images
  • Catch every trick. Word filters can be dodged.
  • Ban anyone automatically. A person decides.

What you get

  • The kit: a double-click setup helper for Mac and Windows, starter game code, and the safety system
  • Plain-English commands: /make to build from an idea, /incidents to review the safety log
  • The guide: one page covering setup, the weekend plan, troubleshooting and a parents' safety section
  • Safety handbook: what to do if you see something, hotlines, and what never to do with evidence
  • Coming: ready-made game templates (obby, home-run derby, team clubhouse), free to buyers when released

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English. The helper handles setup and tells you exactly where to click when it needs you.

Can my kid use it on their own?

No, and that's by design. Claude accounts are for adults (18+). The kit is built for a grown-up and a kid working side by side: they design, you type.

Does this make Roblox safe for my child?

No product can promise that. The kit adds protection inside the game you build together, and gives you tools to notice and report problems. Keep using Roblox's parental controls and stay involved. The guide shows you how.

What computer do I need?

A Mac (macOS 13 or newer) or a Windows 10/11 PC. It doesn't run on Chromebooks, iPads or phones, though the games you make can be played on phones.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email within 7 days for a full refund. No form, no questions.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. It's an independent kit that works with Roblox Studio and Claude Code.
