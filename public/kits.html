How it works

  1. Download, double-click

    "Start Here" installs what's needed and opens a setup helper. No terminal commands to type.

  2. Pick a starter game

    The helper connects to Roblox Studio, adds the game and safety code, makes you the moderator, and tests everything.

  3. Say what you want

    "Add a coin that spins and gives 10 points."

    Claude plans it, builds it and tests it.

It keeps getting better on its own

Leave /loop 1h /adapt running and Claude improves your game every hour while Claude Code is open.

Learns your game

Writes what worked, what broke and how you like to work into a lessons file, and reads it at the start of every session.

Keeps the filter current

Spots new spellings people use to sneak contact requests past the safety filter and adds them. It can only make the filter stricter.

Tunes the difficulty

Reads anonymous play counts (where players fall, quit or breeze through) and adjusts rewards and timings within safe limits.

Playtests and fixes

Starts the game, reads errors, fixes them and tests again. It never touches the safety system.

Questions

Which kit should I get?

Building with a child under 13, or not sure? Get the Safe Build Kit. Building with a teen who wants battles, horror or trading? Get the Teen Build Kit.

Can my child use it on their own?

No. Claude accounts are for adults (18+). A parent or guardian owns the account, runs setup and is the game's safety moderator; the child designs and builds alongside them.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English, and the setup helper tells you exactly where to click when it needs you.

Does this make Roblox safe?

No product can promise that. The kits add protection inside the game you build and tools to notice and report problems. Keep using Roblox's own parental controls.

What computer do I need?

A Mac (macOS 13 or newer) or a Windows 10/11 PC. It doesn't run on Chromebooks, iPads or phones, though the games you make can be played on phones.

How do refunds work?

Reply to your payment receipt within 7 days for a full refund. No form, no questions.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. These are independent kits that work with Roblox Studio and Claude Code, sold through ASH COW.
