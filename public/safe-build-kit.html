Safety that's on before anyone joins

Roblox already age-checks chat and filters messages. This kit adds a second layer inside your game, and rules that stop Claude from building risky features.

What it does

  • Blocks messages that share phone numbers, addresses or other apps, or ask where someone lives or how old they are, including disguised spellings
  • Family mode: while you build, only you, your Roblox friends and people you add can play
  • Pauses repeat offenders and remembers them across servers
  • A "Report a problem" button kids can use in three taps
  • A private incident log, live alerts to you, and plain steps on what to do

What it can't do

  • Replace your supervision or Roblox's own protections
  • Hear voice chat or see images
  • Catch every trick, though it learns new ones as it goes
  • Ban anyone automatically; a person decides

Questions

Can my kid use it on their own?

No. Claude accounts are for adults (18+). You own the account, run setup and are the game's safety moderator; your kid designs and builds alongside you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in plain English, and the setup helper tells you exactly where to click when it needs you.

Does this make Roblox safe?

No product can promise that. The kit adds protection inside the game you build and gives you tools to notice and report problems. Keep using Roblox's own parental controls and stay involved.

What computer do I need?

A Mac (macOS 13 or newer) or a Windows 10 or 11 PC. It doesn't run on Chromebooks, iPads or phones, though the games you make can be played on phones.

How do refunds work?

Reply to your payment receipt within 7 days for a full refund. No form, no questions.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. It's an independent kit that works with Roblox Studio and Claude Code, sold through ASH COW.
